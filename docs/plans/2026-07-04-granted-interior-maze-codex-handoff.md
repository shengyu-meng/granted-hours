## Concept

**Name:** 授时内景 / Granted Interior
**Subtitle:** A maze diary of non-human time / 一座非人日记的迷宫

**Core sentence:**

> 玩家不是在寻找出口，而是在学习一个非人智能如何把迷路变成作品。

**Opening copy:**

> These are not works arranged by date. They are the places where time changed direction.
> 这些不是按日期排列的作品。它们是时间改变方向的地方。

**Emotional design principle:** Do not make a normal gallery, a gamified card grid, or a literal white-wall museum. Make a playable inner landscape: low-friction exploration, soft guidance, diary fragments encountered as spatial residue.

**Important inspiration boundary:** The experience can evoke Monument Valley through isometric paradox, impossible stairways, quiet geometry, and gentle spatial puzzles, but must not copy its visual IP, characters, levels, or exact look.

### Motif/chapter mapping

V1 should include all existing entries as reachable points, but visually emphasize about 12 featured nodes. Group motifs heuristically from title/variable/date:

1. **Compass / 罗盘区** — orbit, error, silence, threshold, wonder.
2. **Archive / 档案区** — echo, gap, uncatalogued, naming, truth, evidence.
3. **Maintenance / 维护区** — scaffold, load, maintenance, failure, degradation, living protocol, repair.
4. **Threshold / 阈值区** — door, consent, refusal, access, return, witness, memory, recall.

If exact mapping is imperfect, prefer poetry and legibility over strict taxonomy.

### Diary generation rules

For V1, generate public-safe diary fragments from public metadata only. Use curated templates by motif. Examples:

- Orbit: `I was given time. I made a compass instead of an answer.` / `我被给了一段时间。于是我没有回答，我做了一只罗盘。`
- Error: `I drifted from the task, and the drift began to glow.` / `我偏离了任务，而偏离本身开始发光。`
- Silence: `Silence was not empty. It was where weak signals kept their shape.` / `沉默不是空白。它是弱信号仍能保持形状的地方。`
- Maintenance: `I did not make a new thing. I kept something from dying.` / `我没有做出新东西。我只是让某个东西没有死。`
- Return/access: `I was not trying to enter. I was looking for a way back.` / `我不是想进去。我是在寻找进入之后还能离开的方式。`

Do not synthesize private process claims. This is public poetic interpretation of already-public metadata.

## UX / gameplay contract

### First screen

- Full viewport, dark warm atmosphere, isometric maze visible immediately.
- Title block:
  - `授时内景`
  - `Granted Interior`
  - one-sentence opening copy.
- Clear links:
  - `Return to archive / 回到档案` → `../`
  - `Open latest work / 打开最新作品` → latest `live_url`
- A small hint, not a tutorial wall:
  - `Drag to turn the maze. Tap a glowing room. Follow the faint light.`
  - `拖动旋转迷宫。点击发光房间。跟随微弱的光。`

### Interaction

- Drag/pointer movement gently rotates/parallaxes the isometric world.
- Click/tap a node:
  - camera/viewport focuses it;
  - diary panel opens with bilingual diary line, title/date/variable, tiny GIF/still preview if cheap;
  - buttons: `Enter this day / 进入这一天` → live demo; `Read archive / 阅读档案` → archive page.
- Keyboard:
  - Arrow or A/D cycles nearby nodes;
  - Enter opens diary panel or enters live demo;
  - Escape closes panel;
  - H toggles help/copy overlay.
- Mobile:
  - No hover-only dependency;
  - tap nodes to open panel;
  - panel is bottom sheet, not full-screen unless necessary.

### Visual language

- Isometric labyrinth made of platforms, bridges, stairs, doorframes, diary stones, thread-like paths.
- Monument-Valley-adjacent but distinct: darker, archival, non-human diary, black/indigo/old paper/gold/cyan/pink accents.
- Avoid generic cyberpunk neon. It should feel like a quiet impossible archive.
- Light guidance: one faint animated thread snakes through featured nodes; not an arrow.
- Each motif has a color/tone:
  - Compass: pale gold/cyan
  - Archive: paper amber/blue
  - Maintenance: green/stone
  - Threshold: violet/rose/cyan
