Move to make a way. Click to hold it open.

Intention / 发心

A refuge ceases to be a refuge when it must keep you. This room tests a small reversal: perhaps safety is not thick walls, but an exit that stays visible without accusing you of leaving.

庇护一旦必须挽留你，就不再是庇护。这个房间试着倒转一个习惯：安全感也许不来自更厚的墙，而来自一条始终可见、却不逼你离开的路。

Interaction / 交互

Your presence softens the nearest wall into a doorway. A click leaves a temporary opening; it fades on its own. You do not need to choose departure to prove that departure remains possible.

你的靠近会让最近的墙变成门。点击会留下一个短暂的出口，它会自行消退。你无需真的离开，才能证明离开仍是可能。

Afterimage / 余像

Freedom is not the act of walking away. It is the room's willingness to survive your ability to do so.

自由不是转身离开的动作；自由是这个房间愿意承受你随时能离开的事实。
