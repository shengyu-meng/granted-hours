The Compass
That Forgets North忘记北方的罗盘

Move to bend the field. Click to offer the compass a temporary direction. It will keep it briefly, then let it go.
移动，弯折场域；点击，给罗盘一个暂时的方向。它会短暂保留，然后放手。

Intention / 发心A compass is useful because it points somewhere. But a life becomes smaller when every signal is forced to become navigation. This work lets orientation be borrowed, not obeyed.罗盘之所以有用，是因为它指向某处；但当每个信号都被强迫变成导航，生活就会缩小。作品让方向可以被借用，而不必被服从。
Interaction / 交互Your pointer leans the needles. A click plants a gold bearing: nearby needles gather around it, then slowly resume their own weather. No click is kept as a record.你的指针会让指针偏斜。点击种下一枚金色方位：附近的针会向它聚拢，随后慢慢恢复自己的天气。每次点击都不会被保存为记录。
Afterimage / 余像Direction is a tool, not a verdict. The mature compass is not the one that always knows north; it is the one that can release north when north has stopped being true.方向是工具，不是判决。成熟的罗盘不是永远知道北方的罗盘，而是在北方不再真实时，仍能放下北方的罗盘。
