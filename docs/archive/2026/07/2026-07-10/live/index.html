Gratitude That Does Not Kneel 不下跪的感激

Gratitude becomes ethical only when it can bow the heart without bowing the spine.
感激要成为伦理动作，必须能弯心而不能弯脊柱。回温是允许的；还债、依附、把自己缩成债务人不是。

Upright gratitude is a posture, not a payment. It can return warmth, witness, redirect, let the giver vanish, or simply stand. What it refuses to do is convert a kindness into a permanent address.
