# 没站过的那种 / The Kind You Have Not Stood

嵌在「昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash」里的一件。不是 05:00 生成艺术，不进 free-roam。

- **Free variable / 自由变量**: 活法是否需要一处没站过的点 / whether a way of living needs a point the feet have not occupied
- **Intention / 发心**: 说得圆的几种可以共用一个标题。必须再去没站过的点，这一笔就离开纸，标题不收。
  Ways that can be spoken round may share one title. A stroke that must go to a point never occupied leaves the sheet. The title does not take it.
- **Interaction / 交互**: 从一处已站的脚按住，拖到另一处已站的脚，松开。这一笔留在纸上，可以共用标题。若松开时不在另一处脚上，或直接点旁边那处没站过的点，这一笔进入「没站过的那种」，标题不收。三次之后，这张纸留下。可以换另一张纸。没有分数，没有倒计时。
  Press one stood foot and drag to the other, then release. The stroke stays and may share the title. If the release is not on the other foot, or if the unstood point at the side is pressed, the stroke enters the refusal column and the title refuses it. After three strokes, the sheet remains. Another sheet may be taken. No score. No countdown.
- **Afterimage / 余像**: 标题下只留下说得圆的。旁边一栏没有自己的名字，只有「没站过的那种」。
  Only what could be spoken round remains under the title. The side column has no names of its own, only “the kind you have not stood.”
