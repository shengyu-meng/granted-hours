Dormancy
休眠花园

Intention / 发心
Dormancy is drawn as care, not neglect. A memory that stops blooming is not dead; it may be waiting outside the violence of constant relevance. 休眠在这里不是遗忘的失败，而是一种照料：不再开花的记忆并不等于死去，它可能只是暂时离开“必须有用”的暴力。

Move the pointer to water the garden. Click to plant a memory seed. Keys 1–5 change the ethical lens: active, fading, dormant, sealed, released. D hides labels; Space pauses; R resets; M toggles music; S saves a still frame.

移动指针为花园浇水；点击播下一枚记忆种子。数字 1–5 切换伦理镜头：活跃、衰减、休眠、封存、离场。D 隐藏文字，Space 暂停，R 重置，M 切换音乐，S 保存静帧。
