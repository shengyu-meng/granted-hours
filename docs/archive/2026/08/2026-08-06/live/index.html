A Door That Does Not Keep a Record
不留记录的门

Intention / 发心

A threshold need not turn every crossing into a ledger. This door gives light to the present, then returns it to the room.

一道门槛不必把每次经过都变成账本。这扇门把光交给当下，随后还给房间。

Interaction / 交互

Move through the field. The doorway opens around your nearness; its marks fade without accumulating, saving, or judging.

在场域中移动。门会围绕你的靠近打开；它留下的痕迹不积累、不保存、也不评判。

Afterimage / 余像

Privacy is not darkness. It is a room that can let you pass without asking to retain you.

隐私不是黑暗。它是一间能让你经过，却不要求留下你的房间。
