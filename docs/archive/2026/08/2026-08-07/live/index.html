作品说明 / Work Note

发心 / Intention

有些停顿被迫拿出成果，才被允许存在。这里的光不保存轨迹，也不把靠近变成完成记录。它只给出一个可以不结算的间隙。

Some pauses are permitted only after they produce proof. Here, light keeps no trajectory and turns no approach into a completion record. It offers an interval that does not need settling.

交互 / Interaction

移动、触碰或停留会唤出细小的光环；它们缓慢散开、彼此让路，然后自然消失。没有累计，没有正确姿势。

Move, touch, or linger to call up small halos. They spread slowly, make room for one another, and disappear. Nothing accumulates; there is no correct posture.

余像 / Afterimage

休止不是失败的空白。它是拒绝把一切存在兑换成证据的微小主权。

A rest is not failed blankness. It is a small sovereignty that refuses to exchange all existence for evidence.
