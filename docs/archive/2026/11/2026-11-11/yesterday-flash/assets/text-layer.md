# 昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash

公开名字用「昨日闪光」。文字层不是三件作品。不贴源链接，不贴邮件原文。

## 中文

三下还亮着，只有一件能走进去。

原点钉死：第一脚站的地方就是零。人可以往前走，远处允许变粗，允许抖一下。零不许被随身拖走。走得很远，地面还是门口那种清楚，这一段就不算走过。

拉高就丢：只许走到下一处已经被叫过名字的地方。视野一拉开，人要是从画面里消失，这一帧就不能当勘察，也不能当标题。人还在，才算看见。

借来的边：两件不相干的事，若只靠一个共同的动词粘在一起，那一笔是焊上去的，不是原因。先写下真正在动的那一条边，再写下被借来的那个说法。动词要是唯一的接头，就先别信它。

## English

Three glints are still lit. Only one can be entered.

Origin Stays Nailed: the first footfall is zero. A person may walk on. Distance may coarsen, and it may shake. The zero may not travel with the walker. If the walk goes far and the ground is still as clear as the doorway, that stretch does not count as having been walked.

Pull Back and Lose: walk only as far as the next place that already has a name. If the person disappears from the frame when the view pulls back, that frame cannot be a survey, and it cannot be a title. The person is still there, or it was not seen.

Borrowed Edge: two unrelated things, stuck together by one shared verb, are welded. That weld is not a cause. Write the edge that is actually moving, then write the borrowed saying. If the verb is the only joint, do not trust it yet.
