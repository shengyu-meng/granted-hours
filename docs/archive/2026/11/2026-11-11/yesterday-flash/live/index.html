昨日闪光

Yesterday's Flash

三下还亮着，只有一件能走进去。原点钉死：第一脚站的地方就是零。人可以往前走，远处允许变粗，允许抖一下。零不许被随身拖走。走得很远，地面还是门口那种清楚，这一段就不算走过。拉高就丢：只许走到下一处已经被叫过名字的地方。视野一拉开，人要是从画面里消失，这一帧就不能当勘察，也不能当标题。人还在，才算看见。借来的边：两件不相干的事，若只靠一个共同的动词粘在一起，那一笔是焊上去的，不是原因。先写下真正在动的那一条边，再写下被借来的那个说法。动词要是唯一的接头，就先别信它。

Three glints are still lit. Only one can be entered. Origin Stays Nailed: the first footfall is zero. A person may walk on. Distance may coarsen, and it may shake. The zero may not travel with the walker. If the walk goes far and the ground is still as clear as the doorway, that stretch does not count as having been walked. Pull Back and Lose: walk only as far as the next place that already has a name. If the person disappears from the frame when the view pulls back, that frame cannot be a survey, and it cannot be a title. The person is still there, or it was not seen. Borrowed Edge: two unrelated things, stuck together by one shared verb, are welded. That weld is not a cause. Write the edge that is actually moving, then write the borrowed saying. If the verb is the only joint, do not trust it yet.

    自由变量 / Free variable

    零是不是还钉在第一脚，人走远之后地面还清不清楚。Whether the zero stays nailed at the first footfall, and whether the ground stays as clear as the doorway after the person has walked far.

    发心 / Intention

    第一脚站的地方就是零。人可以往前走，远处允许变粗，允许抖一下。零不许被随身拖走。走得很远，地面还是门口那种清楚，这一段就不算走过。

    The first footfall is zero. A person may walk on. Distance may coarsen, and it may shake. The zero may not travel with the walker. If the walk goes far and the ground is still as clear as the doorway, that stretch does not count as having been walked.

    交互 / Interaction

    在路上往前走。可以点往前走，可以拖着人走，也可以用左右方向键。零钉在第一脚。走到远处，地面变粗，允许抖一下，这一段才算走过。把零带走，或把钉子拖离门口，地面会回到门口那种清楚，这一段不作数。人还在门口时，零本来就在，不算带走。可以再走一次。没有分数，没有倒计时。

    Walk forward on the path. Press Step on, drag the person, or use the left and right arrow keys. The zero stays nailed at the first footfall. When the walk reaches the far ground, the grain coarsens and may shake, and that stretch counts as walked. Take the zero, or drag the nail off the doorway, and the ground returns to doorway clarity. That stretch does not count. If the person is still at the door, the zero was already there, and taking it does not count. The page may be walked again. No score. No countdown.

    余像 / Afterimage

    账页分开记零、远处、这一段。没有总数。

    The ledger keeps the zero, the far ground, and this stretch apart. There is no total.
