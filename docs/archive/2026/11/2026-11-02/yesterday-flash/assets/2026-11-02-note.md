# 不转头就没有风 / No Wind Without a Turn of the Neck

嵌在「昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash」里的一件。不是 05:00 生成艺术，不进 free-roam。

- **Free variable / 自由变量**: 这一下是绕视，还是转头 / whether the gesture is an orbit or a turn of the neck
- **Intention / 发心**: 在软地里绕完一圈，草若还是直的，就没有风。脖子转过刻度再松开，草才弯。绕视不是转头。
  If a full circle around the soft ground leaves the grass straight, there is no wind. The grass bends only when the neck passes the mark and is released. An orbit is not a turn.
- **Interaction / 交互**: 按住绕视的点，沿圈同一方向拖满一圈再松开；或点「绕一圈」。草不会因此弯。按住脖子上的标记，转向左边或右边，过了刻度再松开；或点「向左转」「向右转」。没过刻度就松开，标记回到正前，没有风。脖子到不了一圈。两种都试过以后，这张纸留下。可以换另一张纸。没有分数，没有倒计时。
  Press the orbit point, drag it a full circle in one direction, and release. Or press the circle control. The grass does not bend from this. Press the mark on the neck, turn it left or right, and release past the mark. Or press the left-turn or right-turn control. Releasing before the mark returns the facing forward, and there is no wind. The neck cannot complete a circle. After both gestures are tried, the sheet remains. Another sheet may be taken. No score. No countdown.
- **Afterimage / 余像**: 账页分开记绕视和转头。绕完的，草还是直的，不记成风。转过刻度的，留下弯过的草。
  The ledger keeps the orbit and the turn apart. A completed circle leaves the grass straight and is not recorded as wind. A turn past the mark leaves the bent grass.
