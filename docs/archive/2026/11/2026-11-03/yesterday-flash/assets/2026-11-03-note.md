# 沉到底才算地 / Ground Only After It Sinks

嵌在「昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash」里的一件。不是 05:00 生成艺术，不进 free-roam。

- **Free variable / 自由变量**: 第一句有没有沉到底，并且之后没有被改 / whether the first sentence has sunk to the bed and stayed unedited
- **Intention / 发心**: 第一句沉到底并且不再改，这一页才可以叫地。改了第一句，这一页回到草稿。闪开不是沉底。
  The page may be called ground only when the first sentence has sunk and is not revised. Editing the first sentence returns the page to draft. Stepping aside is not sinking.
- **Interaction / 交互**: 按住第一句，向下拖过底线再松开；或点「沉到底」。没过底线就松开，句子回到水上，还不能叫地。锁住以后，点「写在旁边」，下一句只出现在旁边，第一句不动。点「闪开」，或把句子往旁边拖开再松开：句子让开，仍是草稿；已经锁住的不让开。点「改第一句」：若已经沉底，锁打开，句子改过，这一页回到草稿，旁边那句撤回。可以再沉一次。三种都试过以后，这张纸留下。可以换另一张纸。没有分数，没有倒计时。
  Press the first sentence, drag it down past the bed line, and release. Or press Sink. Releasing above the line returns the sentence to the water. It cannot yet be called ground. After it locks, press Write beside. The next sentence appears only beside it. The first sentence does not move. Press Step aside, or drag the sentence aside and release: it yields, and the page stays a draft. A locked sentence does not step aside. Press Edit the first sentence: if it has sunk, the lock opens, the wording changes, the page returns to draft, and the beside-sentence is withdrawn. It may sink again. After all three gestures are tried, the sheet remains. Another sheet may be taken. No score. No countdown.
- **Afterimage / 余像**: 账页分开记沉底、旁边、闪开、改第一句。沉到底且没改的，才记成地。改过的，记成回到草稿。让开的，不记成地。
  The ledger keeps the sink, the beside-line, the step aside, and the edit apart. Only a sunk and unedited sentence is recorded as ground. An edit is recorded as a return to draft. A yield is not recorded as ground.
