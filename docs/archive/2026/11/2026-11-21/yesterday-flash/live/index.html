昨日闪光

Yesterday's Flash

三下还亮着，只有一件能走进去。一起转的平面：从脚底到树冠切开，每层只留那一高度真正切到的东西。换方向只能让所有层转同一个角度。唯一允许动手的地方，是让其中一层少转十度。少转的那一层露馅。露馅以后，纸必须承认，你以为站得住的山，是一组被要求一起转的平面。没有一层少转，这页不能收。灯必须动过：纸上只写三样允许被改的实物，灯的高度，缝的宽，墙的转角。进来的人只能改这三样，再走同一条路。判断变了，这三样却还在原来的位置，这张纸作废。没有一件东西真的被拧过，涨了的判断不算数。跟着进门的：每走一步，只写下下一间用不上、却还是跟进来的那一件，并写出名字。什么都没跟进来，这步还没走完。上一间整间都跟进来，也不算，那是搬家。名字要具体到能指着它说，就是这个。

Three glints are still lit. Only one can be entered. Co-rotated Planes: cut from the foot to the crown. Each layer may keep only what that height actually cuts. A change of direction may turn every layer only by the same angle. The only allowed hand is to let one layer lag by ten degrees. That layer gives the trick away. Afterward the page must admit that the mountain you thought could stand is a set of planes required to turn together. If no layer lags, the page cannot be kept. The Lamp Must Have Moved: the page may name only three physical things that may be changed, the height of the lamp, the width of the seam, the corner of the wall. The person who enters may change only these three, then walk the same path again. If the judgment has changed and the three are still where they were, the page is void. If nothing was actually turned, a risen judgment does not count. What Followed In: each step may write only the one thing the next room does not need and that still came in, and it must be named. If nothing followed in, the step is not finished. If the whole previous room followed in, that does not count. That is moving house. The name must be specific enough to point at.

      Work note / 作品说明

      一起转的平面

      从脚底到树冠切开，每层只留那一高度真正切到的东西。点一层，或用左右方向键选中。按全体左转或全体右转，六层转同一个角度。按少转十度，或用上方向键，只许一层少转。按收回少转，或用下方向键。按山自报，或按回车，只在你正指着少转的那一层时。按收下这一页。没有少转不能收。露馅了却没自报，这页作废。没有分数，没有倒计时。故意不发声。

      Cut from the foot to the crown. Each layer keeps only what that height cuts. Select a layer by pressing it, or with the left and right arrow keys. Press Turn all left or Turn all right. Six layers turn by the same angle. Press Lag by ten degrees, or use the up arrow. Only one layer may lag. Press Withdraw the lag, or use the down arrow. Press Let the mountain confess, or press Enter, only while pointing at the lagged layer. Press Keep this page. If nothing lags, the page cannot be kept. If the trick has shown and the mountain has not confessed, the page is void. No score. No countdown. Deliberate silence.
