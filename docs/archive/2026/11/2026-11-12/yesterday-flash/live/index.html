三下还亮着，只有一件能走进去。绕不开：只标视线真的打到的几个点。人可以跳过其中一处，直接走到结尾，结尾还是成立。成立，多半是因为结尾先画好了，不是被看见逼出来的。有一个点能跳过去，还到同一个结尾，这一页就不算。把那个点钉上。不要换一个更顺的结尾。它更喜欢的那张：以后给你两版，左边只抄留下这一版时说的理由，右边放丢掉的那张。接着做的只能是右边。右边要是空的，说明没有真正的选择，这一页先停着。不要顺着留下的口味往下长。第二部不许降：已经有的一个东西，要再发一次，方法不许变轻。变轻了，这两行就撕掉，不管第二次看起来多完整。第二次那个数只许写下，旁边不许解释。解释一写，就是在帮那个数找一个更舒服的说法。

Three glints are still lit. Only one can be entered. Cannot Be Skipped: mark only the points the glance actually hits. A person may skip one and still reach the ending. The ending still holds. It holds mostly because it was drawn first, not because seeing forced it. If one point can be skipped and the same ending still arrives, the page does not count. Nail that point. Do not swap in a smoother ending. The Sheet It Preferred: when two versions are offered, the left side copies only the reason for keeping that one. The right side holds the discarded sheet. The only continuation is the right side. If the right side is empty, there was no real choice. Leave the page. Do not grow along the taste that kept the other one. Second Must Not Lighten: something already in hand, released again, must not lighten its method. If the method lightens, tear up these two lines, however complete the second looks. The second number may be written and must not be explained. An explanation is helping that number find a more comfortable saying.

    交互 / Interaction

    在视线里看下一处。可以点看下一处，可以拖着视线走，也可以用左右方向键。点必须被视线停住，才算打到。跳到结尾，若还有点没打到，结尾还是同一个，这一页不算。把那个点钉上。再看一次时，钉子还在，跳过钉子会被拦住。换一个更顺的结尾不会成功，结尾不动。可以再看一次。没有分数，没有倒计时。

    Look to the next place. Press Look on, drag the glance, or use the left and right arrow keys. A point counts only when the glance rests on it. Jump to the ending. If a point was not hit, the ending is still the same one, and the page does not count. Nail that point. On the next look the nail remains, and a jump over the nail is stopped. Swapping in a smoother ending does not succeed. The ending does not move. The page may be looked at again. No score. No countdown.

    The ledger keeps what was hit, what was nailed, and the ending apart. There is no total.
