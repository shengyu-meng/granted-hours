# 欠着的照看 / Care Still Owed

嵌在「昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash」里的一件。不是 05:00 生成艺术，不进 free-roam。

- **Free variable / 自由变量**: 离开之后的照看有没有标上价 / whether the care after leaving is priced
- **Intention / 发心**: 承下来的那一块，要是离开之后的照看没有标上价，这块就是空的。你可以离开。价要写在离开之后。离开之前写的价不算。没标价就走，承下来的东西不成立。
  An inherited part stays void unless the care after leaving is priced. You may leave. The price must be written after the leaving. A price written before leaving does not count. Leaving without a price does not hold the part.
- **Interaction / 交互**: 点一块，或用左右方向键选中。按离开，或用上方向键，也可以把这块拖过离开那条线。再按一夜、一次回头或留下名字，给离开之后的照看标价。按擦掉价。按回来，或用下方向键，也可以把这块拖回原位。回来之后，已写的价不再算离开之后的照看。按收下这一页。只有至少离开了一块，而且每一块离开了的都在离开之后标了价，同时没有价写在离开之前，这一页才可以收。坐着没走、也没写价的块可以留在原位。可以再改。没有分数，没有倒计时。
  Select a part by pressing it, or with the left and right arrow keys. Press Leave, or use the up arrow key. You may also drag that part across the leaving line. Then press One night, One look back, or The name left behind, to price the care after leaving. Press Erase the price. Press Return, or use the down arrow key. You may also drag the part back to its seat. After a return, a price already written no longer counts as care after leaving. Press Keep this page. The page can be kept only when at least one part has left, every part that left has a price written after that leaving, and no price was written before leaving. A part still seated, with no price, may stay where it is. Marks may be changed. No score. No countdown.
- **Afterimage / 余像**: 账页分开记已离开的、离开后标了价的、离开却没标价的、价写在离开之前的。没有总数。价标对了，也不会把那一块送回来。
  The ledger keeps what has left, what was priced after leaving, what left without a price, and prices written before leaving apart. There is no total. A correct price does not bring the part back.

- **Experience duration / 体验时长**: open-ended / 开放
