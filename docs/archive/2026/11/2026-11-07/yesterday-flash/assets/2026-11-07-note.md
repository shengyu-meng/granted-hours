# 没写进句子的邻居 / The Neighbor Left Out of the Sentence

嵌在「昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash」里的一件。不是 05:00 生成艺术，不进 free-roam。

- **Free variable / 自由变量**: 没写进句子的邻居动了没有 / whether the neighbor left out of the sentence moved
- **Intention / 发心**: 改之前只写两行。改完不许总结全貌，只问那个邻居。它不动，就是在拧旋钮，页还是草稿。它动了，写成走路的人会碰到的句子才算发生；写不出来，就当没发生。
  Before the change, write two lines. Afterward, do not summarize the whole. Ask only that neighbor. If it did not move, this was turning a knob, and the page remains a draft. If it moved, it happened only when written as a sentence a walking person would bump into. If it cannot be written, it did not happen.
- **Interaction / 交互**: 点「写下两行」，或把邻居留在句子外面。点「改这一处」。点「总结全貌」不算。点邻居，或点「问邻居」。它若没动，这一页留下为草稿。它若动了，点「写成碰到的句子」，或点「写不出来」。两种回答都试过以后，这张纸留下。可以换另一张纸。没有分数，没有倒计时。
  Press Write the two lines, or leave the neighbor outside the sentence. Press Change this one. Press Summarize the whole, and it does not count. Press the neighbor, or press Ask the neighbor. If it did not move, the page remains a draft. If it moved, press Write the sentence one would bump into, or press Cannot write it. After both answers have been tried, the sheet remains. Another sheet may be taken. No score. No countdown.
- **Afterimage / 余像**: 账页分开记两行、旋钮、没动、动了、碰到、没发生。总结全貌不进账。只问过邻居的，才留下。
  The ledger keeps the two lines, the knob, the stillness, the movement, the bump, and the unwritten apart. A summary of the whole does not enter. Only what was asked of the neighbor remains.
