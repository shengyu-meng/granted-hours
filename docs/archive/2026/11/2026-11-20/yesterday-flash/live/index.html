昨日闪光

Yesterday's Flash

三下还亮着，只有一件能走进去。替身半径：脚底下那一小块才许有量过的东西。眼睛还看得到、人还没走到的，必须自报是替身，并写出缺的那块骨头。来的人指不出这条线，这页作废。换一天就死：当时走通的办法，换一个普通日子就会空。写不出这个普通日子，那次赢不许记成方法。回不来的：带出来的只许放在自己桌上。必须单写一件碰过、一拿出来就死掉的。没有这件，这页只是一次成功的搬运。

Three glints are still lit. Only one can be entered. The Stand-in Radius: only the small patch underfoot may hold what was measured. What the eye still sees, and no one has walked to, must confess it is a stand-in and name the missing bone. If the person who arrives cannot point out this line, the page is void. Dies If the Day Changes: a way that worked that day goes empty on an ordinary day. If that ordinary day cannot be written, the win may not be kept as a method. What Cannot Come Back: what is carried out may only be what can sit on your own table. One touched thing that dies the moment it is taken out must be written alone. Without it, the page is only a successful carrying.

      Work note / 作品说明

      替身半径

      脚底下那一小块才许有量过的东西。眼睛还看得到、人还没走到的，必须自报是替身，并写出缺的那块骨头。拖动脚，或按往西、往东、往北、往南，线跟着脚走。点一处，或用左右方向键选中。按记下量过，或用上方向键，只在脚底下记得住。远处按自报替身，或在远处时用上方向键。量过的一处若离开脚底，按收回这一量，或用下方向键，再让它自报。点跟着脚走的那条线，或聚焦后按回车。眼睛的圈不算。没有分数，没有倒计时。故意不发声。

      Only the small patch underfoot may hold what was measured. What the eye still sees, and no one has walked to, must confess it is a stand-in and name the missing bone. Drag the foot, or press West, East, North, South. The line follows the foot. Select a place by pressing it, or with the left and right arrow keys. Press Record as measured, or use the up arrow, only while it is underfoot. Far away, press Confess as stand-in, or use the up arrow. If a measured place leaves the foot, press Withdraw this measurement, or use the down arrow, then let it confess. Point the line that follows the foot, or focus it and press Enter. The eye's circle does not count. No score. No countdown. Deliberate silence.
