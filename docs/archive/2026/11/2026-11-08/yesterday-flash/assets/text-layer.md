# 昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash

公开名字用「昨日闪光」。文字层不是三件作品。不贴源链接，不贴邮件原文。

## 中文

三下还亮着，只有一下能走进去。体积不许减：过之前写一个体积，过之后再写一个，两个数必须一样。变的只能是厚度从哪条轴让出来、又被哪条轴接住。这句话要用走路的人碰得到的话说。体积要是小了，这一页还是缩小声明，不许叫通过。皮肤的年份：只挑一块砖，写下地的年份和皮的年份。地是测绘发生的那一年，皮是这套画法属于的十年，两个数不许相同。再写一句只有站在其中一年才说得通、走路的人会用的话。两个年份怎么写都一样，这张图还是借了地的钟的贴图，还没变成场地。算在自己身上：只准用这块地自己的颗粒量一次。失败也写在卡上，失败就是这一次的结果。另一间房里更细的那次划掉，旁边注明另一间房，不许用它来证明这块地算成了。

## English

Three glints are still lit; only one can be entered.
Volume Must Not Shrink: before the passage, write a volume. After it, write another. The two numbers must be the same. Only the thickness may change, and only by naming which axis yielded it and which axis received it. Say that in words a walking person would bump into. If the volume got smaller, the page is still a shrinking statement, and it may not be called a passage.
The Year of the Skin: pick one tile. Write the year of the ground and the year of the skin. The ground's year is when the survey happened. The skin's year is the decade the drawing method belongs to. The two numbers must not match. Then write one sentence that makes sense only when standing in one of those years, a sentence a walking person would use. If the years always come out the same, the image is still a texture that borrowed the ground's clock. It is not yet a site.
Reckoned on Its Own Body: measure once, only at this site's own grain. Write the failure on the card. The failure is the result. Cross out the finer measurement from the other room, mark it as the other room, and do not use it to prove that this site computed.
