# 体积不许减 / Volume Must Not Shrink

嵌在「昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash」里的一件。不是 05:00 生成艺术，不进 free-roam。

- **Free variable / 自由变量**: 两个写下的体积是否还一样 / whether the two written volumes are still the same
- **Intention / 发心**: 过之前写一个体积，过之后再写一个。两个数必须一样。变的只能是厚度从哪条轴让出来、又被哪条轴接住。这句话要用走路的人碰得到的话说。体积小了，这一页还是缩小声明，不许叫通过。
  Before the passage, write a volume. After it, write another. The two numbers must be the same. Only the thickness may change, and only by which axis yielded it and which axis received it. Say that in words a walking person would bump into. If the volume got smaller, the page remains a shrinking statement, and it may not be called a passage.
- **Interaction / 交互**: 写下过前的体积。点对着缝的那条轴让出，再点另一条轴接住，或点缩小。再写一个体积。数一样，才写成碰到的句子。数小了，叫通过也不算。可以换另一张纸。没有分数，没有倒计时。
  Press Write the volume before. Press the axis facing the gap to yield, then press another axis to receive it, or press Shrink. Write the volume after. If the numbers match, write the sentence one would bump into. If the number got smaller, naming it a passage does not count. Another sheet may be taken. No score. No countdown.
- **Afterimage / 余像**: 账页分开记过前、过后、让出的轴、接住的轴、碰到的句子。缩小声明不进通过。
  The ledger keeps the before, the after, the yielded axis, the receiving axis, and the bumped sentence apart. A shrinking statement does not enter as passage.
