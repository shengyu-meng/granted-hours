昨日闪光

Yesterday's Flash

三下还亮着，只有一件能走进去。报不了价的：一张图要是每根线都能换成数量和价钱，它可以去当报价单，不该再当一块地的名字。只写下报不了价的东西，一条没有面积的路，一个没有体积的门槛，一个没有材料的看。页是空的，说明那张图本来就是清单，只是穿着平面图的衣服。再量细一点，每一条都能变成长度或面积，这一页先别收。只是还没量完。原文如此：干净的那一栏可以编号，可以翻译，可以核对，不许改掉原文里写错的原因、写错的名字、写错的转向。错的地方两栏都留着。干净的那一栏要是一个都没有，整理已经把本子吃掉了。涂掉一个错转向，那一次走就换了一条路。谁在等：每次停顿只标三种，人在等机器，机器在等人，两边都还没收住。前两种只是记账。第三种才要写下那一下场地变了什么，一步，一扇门，或一句没说完的话。一种重叠都没有，就写这周没有重叠，别补一场。

Three glints are still lit. Only one can be entered. What Cannot Be Priced: a drawing whose every line can become quantity and price may be a quotation. It should not name a site. Write only what cannot be priced, a path with no area, a threshold with no volume, a look with no material. An empty page means the drawing was a bill wearing a plan's clothes. Measure finer until every line becomes length or area, and do not keep the page. The measuring is not finished. As Written: the clean column may number, translate, and check. It may not repair the wrong cause, the wrong name, or the wrong turn in the original. The wrong place stays in both columns. If the clean column has none, the notebook has already been eaten. Erase one wrong turn, and that walk becomes another path. Who Is Waiting: mark each pause as one of three, a person waiting on a machine, a machine waiting on a person, or both still unclosed. The first two are only a ledger. The third must say what the site changed in that moment, a step, a door, or a sentence left unfinished. If there is no overlap, write that this week had none. Do not invent one.

    报不了价的

    What Cannot Be Priced

    自由变量 / Free variable

    这条线被标成价钱，还是报不了价。Whether a line is marked price or unsellable.

    发心 / Intention

    一张图要是每根线都能换成数量和价钱，它可以去当报价单，不该再当一块地的名字。只写下报不了价的东西。页是空的，这张图就是清单，穿着平面图的衣服。每一条都量成数量，这一页先别收。图不许给场地起名。

    A drawing whose every line can become quantity and price may be a quotation. It should not name a site. Write only what cannot be priced. An empty page means the drawing was a bill wearing a plan's clothes. If every line becomes quantity, do not keep the page. The drawing may not title the site.

    交互 / Interaction

    点一条线，或用左右方向键选中。再按标价钱，或标报不了价。全标价钱，这一页先别收。按再量细一点，路不会得到面积，门槛不会得到体积，看不会得到材料。墙和地面可以量成数量，然后只去报价单。按用这张图起名不会成功。至少留一条报不了价的，这一页才可以收。可以再改标记。没有分数，没有倒计时。

    Select a line by pressing it, or with the left and right arrow keys. Then press Mark price, or Mark unsellable. Mark every line a price, and the page is not kept. Press Measure finer, and the path does not gain area, the threshold does not gain volume, and the look does not gain material. The wall and the ground may become quantity, and then they go only to the quotation. Press Name the site with this drawing, and it does not succeed. The page can be kept only when at least one line is marked unsellable. Marks may be changed. No score. No countdown.

    余像 / Afterimage

    账页分开记价钱、报不了价、场地名。没有总数。

    The ledger keeps price, unsellable, and the site name apart. There is no total.
