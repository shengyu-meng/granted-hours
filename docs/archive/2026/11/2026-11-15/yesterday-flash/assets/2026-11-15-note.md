# 报不了价的 / What Cannot Be Priced

嵌在「昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash」里的一件。不是 05:00 生成艺术，不进 free-roam。

- **Free variable / 自由变量**: 这条线被标成价钱，还是报不了价 / whether a line is marked price or unsellable
- **Intention / 发心**: 一张图要是每根线都能换成数量和价钱，它可以去当报价单，不该再当一块地的名字。只写下报不了价的东西，一条没有面积的路，一个没有体积的门槛，一个没有材料的看。页是空的，说明那张图本来就是清单，只是穿着平面图的衣服。再量细一点，每一条都能变成长度或面积，这一页先别收。只是还没量完。图不许给场地起名。
  A drawing whose every line can become quantity and price may be a quotation. It should not name a site. Write only what cannot be priced, a path with no area, a threshold with no volume, a look with no material. An empty page means the drawing was a bill wearing a plan's clothes. Measure finer until every line becomes length or area, and do not keep the page. The measuring is not finished. The drawing may not title the site.
- **Interaction / 交互**: 点一条线，或用左右方向键选中。再按标价钱，或标报不了价。全标价钱，这一页先别收。按再量细一点，路不会得到面积，门槛不会得到体积，看不会得到材料。墙和地面可以量成数量，然后只去报价单。按用这张图起名不会成功，场地名还是没有。至少留一条报不了价的，这一页才可以收。可以再改标记。没有分数，没有倒计时。
  Select a line by pressing it, or with the left and right arrow keys. Then press Mark price, or Mark unsellable. Mark every line a price, and the page is not kept. Press Measure finer, and the path does not gain area, the threshold does not gain volume, and the look does not gain material. The wall and the ground may become quantity, and then they go only to the quotation. Press Name the site with this drawing, and it does not succeed. The site name stays none. The page can be kept only when at least one line is marked unsellable. Marks may be changed. No score. No countdown.
- **Afterimage / 余像**: 账页分开记价钱、报不了价、场地名。没有总数。留下报不了价的，也不会让这张图给场地起名。
  The ledger keeps price, unsellable, and the site name apart. There is no total. Keeping what cannot be priced does not let the drawing title the site.
