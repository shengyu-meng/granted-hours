# 昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash

公开名字用「昨日闪光」。文字层不是三件作品。不贴源链接，不贴邮件原文。

## 中文

三下还亮着，只有一件能走进去。

报不了价的：一张图要是每根线都能换成数量和价钱，它可以去当报价单，不该再当一块地的名字。只写下报不了价的东西，一条没有面积的路，一个没有体积的门槛，一个没有材料的看。页是空的，说明那张图本来就是清单，只是穿着平面图的衣服。再量细一点，每一条都能变成长度或面积，这一页先别收。只是还没量完。

原文如此：干净的那一栏可以编号，可以翻译，可以核对，不许改掉原文里写错的原因、写错的名字、写错的转向。错的地方两栏都留着。干净的那一栏要是一个都没有，整理已经把本子吃掉了。涂掉一个错转向，那一次走就换了一条路。

谁在等：每次停顿只标三种，人在等机器，机器在等人，两边都还没收住。前两种只是记账。第三种才要写下那一下场地变了什么，一步，一扇门，或一句没说完的话。一种重叠都没有，就写这周没有重叠，别补一场。

## English

Three glints are still lit. Only one can be entered.

What Cannot Be Priced: a drawing whose every line can become quantity and price may be a quotation. It should not name a site. Write only what cannot be priced, a path with no area, a threshold with no volume, a look with no material. An empty page means the drawing was a bill wearing a plan's clothes. Measure finer until every line becomes length or area, and do not keep the page. The measuring is not finished.

As Written: the clean column may number, translate, and check. It may not repair the wrong cause, the wrong name, or the wrong turn in the original. The wrong place stays in both columns. If the clean column has none, the notebook has already been eaten. Erase one wrong turn, and that walk becomes another path.

Who Is Waiting: mark each pause as one of three, a person waiting on a machine, a machine waiting on a person, or both still unclosed. The first two are only a ledger. The third must say what the site changed in that moment, a step, a door, or a sentence left unfinished. If there is no overlap, write that this week had none. Do not invent one.
