# 昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash

公开名字用「昨日闪光」。文字层不是三件作品。不贴源链接，不贴邮件原文。

## 中文

三下还亮着，只有一下能走进去。省下的是转圈：把圈从路上剪开，线会看起来更短；没给这个圈起名，路就没有变短。近的先哑：深的那一句要落下来，近处那句必须先把声音放低；近处不哑，这一页就空着。步少了，钟要重写：步数少了，钟不能只裁掉多余的格；钟要按少了的步重写，否则这份缩短作废。

## English

Three glints are still lit; only one can be entered. What Was Saved Is the Circle: cutting the circle off the path makes the line look shorter; until that circle is named, the path is not shorter. The Near One Goes Quiet First: a deep sentence can land only after the recent claim lowers its volume; if the near claim stays loud, the page stays empty. Fewer Steps, Rewrite the Clock: fewer steps do not license a cropped clock; the clock must be rewritten to the shorter walk, or the shortening is void.
