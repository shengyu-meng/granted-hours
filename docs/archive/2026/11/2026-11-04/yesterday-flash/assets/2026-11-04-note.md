# 省下的是转圈 / What Was Saved Is the Circle

嵌在「昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash」里的一件。不是 05:00 生成艺术，不进 free-roam。

- **Free variable / 自由变量**: 被剪开的圈有没有名字 / whether the cut circle has a name
- **Intention / 发心**: 省下只在圈被起名以后才算数。只把圈剪开，线可以看起来更短，路没有变短。
  The saving counts only after the circle is named. Cutting the circle may make the line look shorter. The path is not shorter.
- **Interaction / 交互**: 按住转圈，向上拖过抬起线再松开；或点「剪开」。没过线就松开，圈回到路上，路没有变短。剪开以后，线会看起来更短，账上的步数不动。把一个名字拖进缺口，或点那个名字。名字没放进缺口，圈仍未名，路没有变短。名字落下以后，账上的步数才跟着短，圈留在旁边，带着那个名字。两种都试过以后，这张纸留下。可以换另一张纸。没有分数，没有倒计时。
  Press the circle, drag it up past the lift line, and release. Or press Cut. Releasing below the line returns the circle to the path. The path is not shorter. After the cut, the line looks shorter and the ledger count does not move. Drag a name into the gap, or press that name. If the name misses the gap, the circle stays unnamed and the path is not shorter. Once the name lands, the ledger count shortens with the line, and the circle remains beside the path, carrying that name. After both gestures are tried, the sheet remains. Another sheet may be taken. No score. No countdown.
- **Afterimage / 余像**: 账页分开记剪开和起名。只剪开的，记下未名，路没有变短。起了名的，圈留在旁边，省下的才记成这一圈。
  The ledger keeps the cut and the naming apart. A cut alone is recorded as unnamed, and the path is not shorter. A named circle remains beside the path, and the saving is recorded as that circle.
