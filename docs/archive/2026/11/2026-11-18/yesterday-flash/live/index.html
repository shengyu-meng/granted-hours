Yesterday's Flash

三下还亮着，只有一件能走进去。没通知的改口：没被通知的那个人，必须说出一个旧规则产不出来的动作。旧规则自己能做的动作，不算改口。先把新说法告诉他，也不算没通知。白送的步：已经走完的步，如果一个知情的本地人本来就会把这些步白送出去，这些步就不算理解。不是没走完。是走完了，却可以被白送。把气口还回去：还回去的气口，如果它多加了一个事实，或者吸不进去，这一口就是空的。不是没还。是还了，却不能被吸，或者多带了一件事实。

Three glints are still lit. Only one can be entered. The Untold Change of Mouth: the person who was not told must name an action the old rule could not produce. An action the old rule could already make does not count as a change of mouth. Telling them the new wording first does not count as untold. Steps Given Away: completed steps are void as understanding if an informed local would have given those steps away. It is not that the steps were unfinished. They were finished, and still could have been given away. Breath Given Back: a restored breath is void if it adds a fact or cannot be inhaled. It is not that nothing was returned. It was returned, and still cannot be inhaled, or it carried one extra fact.

    Work note / 作品说明

    没通知的改口

    没被通知的那个人，必须说出一个旧规则产不出来的动作。点一个人，或用左右方向键选中。按走出纸外，或用上方向键，也可以把这个人拖过纸边。再按把没写过的地方叫成一步。按再走已有的一步。按通知。按闭上嘴。按擦掉这句话，或用下方向键，也可以把这个人拖回纸上。按收下这一页。只有至少一个人没被通知，并且说出了旧规则产不出来的动作，同时没有人正被通知着，这一页才可以收。可以再改。没有分数，没有倒计时。故意不发声。

    The person who was not told must name an action the old rule could not produce. Select a person by pressing them, or with the left and right arrow keys. Press Step off the paper, or use the up arrow key. You may also drag that person across the paper's edge. Then press Name an unwritten place as a step. Press Walk one written step again. Press Notify. Press Seal the mouth. Press Erase this sentence, or use the down arrow key. You may also drag that person back onto the paper. Press Keep this page. The page can be kept only when at least one person was not told, that person named an action the old rule could not produce, and no one is currently being told. Marks may be changed. No score. No countdown. Deliberate silence.
