# 昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash

公开名字用「昨日闪光」。文字层不是三件作品。不贴源链接，不贴邮件原文。

## 中文

三下还亮着，只有一下能走进去。图不许知道：尺寸先写在图外面，这张图永远不许学会它。哪天图把这个数说出来，图就退出，只留一句它从一开始就在等图纸。分不了类的那一镜：别的格子可以写做法，有一格的做法栏不许填，这一格才算作品。哪天这一格也被填上，这一页就退出，只留一句全部分好了，所以不是作品。删掉就没人：只留一句压缩一定会删掉的话，这句不添加事实，只让一个特定的人还在页上。删掉以后论证还在、人也还在，就是气氛。论证还在、人没了，这句才留下。

## English

Three glints are still lit; only one can be entered.
The Image Must Not Know: the measure is written outside the image, and the image may never learn it. The day the image speaks that number, the image leaves, and only the sentence remains that it was waiting for the drawing from the start.
The Unclassifiable Shot: other cells may name a method, and one cell must leave its method blank. That blank cell is the work. The day the blank cell is filled, the page leaves, and only the sentence remains that everything was classified, so it is not a work.
Delete It and No One Remains: one sentence is left that compression will cut. It adds no fact. It only keeps a particular person on the page. If the cut leaves the argument and the person, it was atmosphere. If the argument remains and the person is gone, that sentence is the one that stays.
