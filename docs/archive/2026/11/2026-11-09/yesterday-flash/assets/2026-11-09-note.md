- **Free variable / 自由变量**: 标题是不是只有缺席，撤掉的错因还钉不钉在正文里 / whether the title is only the absence, and whether the retracted cause is still nailed in the body
- **Intention / 发心**: 标题只许写不再应的那一位。原因另起一行，标明还没换。不许升成这夜的名字，也不许从正文里擦掉。原因那一句可以塌，缺席还钉在身上。
  The title may name only who no longer answers. The cause begins on another line, marked not yet replaced. It may not rise into the night's name, and it may not be erased from the body. The cause sentence may collapse. The absence stays nailed in the body.

  Press Write who no longer answers. Nail the wrong cause into the body and mark it not yet replaced. Retract that cause sentence, or try to promote it into the night's name, or try to erase it from the body. The page may stop only when the absence is the title, the cause sentence has collapsed, and the nail remains. A promotion or an erasure does not count. Another night may be taken. No score. No countdown.

  The ledger keeps the title, the cause line, the nail in the body, the refused promotion, and the refused erasure apart. A collapsed cause does not enter the title.
