昨日闪光

Yesterday's Flash

三下还亮着，只有一下能走进去。错因钉着：标题只许写不再应的那一位。原因另起一行，标明还没换。不许升成这夜的名字，也不许从正文里擦掉。原因那一句可以塌，缺席还钉在身上。嘴里的路：上半张只看结构，写一句这地方在玩什么。下半张写后来知道那里实际有的。两句都留，不涂。上半张若是一张嘴，下半张若是一条路，这张卡才算写成。吊不进去：四角各写它们同意的那一句。中间写那个低到让整套装不进去的事实。再写一句因为这个，同意进不了这间屋。没有那个事实就空着，不要发明一根梁来凑。

Three glints are still lit; only one can be entered. Cause Stays Nailed: the title may name only who no longer answers. The cause begins on another line and is marked not yet replaced. It may not rise into the night's name, and it may not be erased from the body. The cause sentence may collapse. The absence stays nailed in the body. Road in the Mouth: the upper half looks only at structure and writes what this place seems to be playing. The lower half writes what was later known to be there. Both sentences stay. Neither is painted over. If the upper half is a mouth and the lower half is a road, the card is written. Will Not Hang In: each corner writes the sentence it agreed to. The center writes the fact low enough that the whole rig will not fit. Then write one sentence, that because of this the agreement cannot enter the room. If that fact is absent, leave the center empty. Do not invent a beam to make it fit.

    错因钉着

    Cause Stays Nailed

    标题只许写不再应的那一位。原因另起一行，标明还没换。不许升成这夜的名字，也不许从正文里擦掉。原因那一句可以塌，缺席还钉在身上。

    The title may name only who no longer answers. The cause stays on its own line, marked not yet replaced. It may not become the night's name, and it may not be erased. The sentence may collapse. The nail remains.

    写下谁不再应。钉住错因。撤掉原因那一句，或试着升名，或试着擦掉。没有分数，没有倒计时。故意不发声。
