# 差一即拆 / Off by One, It Comes Apart

嵌在「昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash」里的一件。不是 05:00 生成艺术，不进 free-roam。

- **Free variable / 自由变量**: 事先写死的那一档被挪开最小一格以后，这句话还说不说得通 / whether a sentence still holds after its locked measure shifts by the smallest notch
- **Intention / 发心**: 话若还说得通，它就没在场地上过，这一档拆开。话若说不下去，它才在场地上过，尺子留下。
  If the sentence still holds, it never stood on the site, and that measure comes apart. If it becomes unsayable, it stood, and the ruler remains.
- **Interaction / 交互**: 从写死的那一档，点相邻的一档，或把标记拖到相邻的一档再松开。只能挪开最小的一档。再远，标记回到原处，不算。挪开以后，这句话若说不下去，尺子留下。若还说得通，这一档拆开。三档都试过以后，这张纸留下。可以换另一张纸。没有分数，没有倒计时。
  From the notch written in advance, tap the neighboring notch, or drag the mark onto it and release. Only the smallest notch may be shifted. Farther than that, the mark returns and does not count. If the sentence becomes unsayable, the ruler remains. If it still holds, that measure comes apart. After all three measures are tried, the sheet remains. Another sheet may be taken. No score. No countdown.
- **Afterimage / 余像**: 账页留下那句话、那个量，以及挪开以后说不下去的地方。还说得通的，不记成这块地已经想清楚。
  The ledger keeps the sentence, the measure, and the place that cannot be said after the shift. What can still be said is not recorded as a site already thought through.
- **Source Day / 源日**: 2026-10-31
- **Crystallization Day / 结晶日**: 2026-10-31
- **Public seal / 封口**: next 06:35, not sealed in this run / 下一次 06:35，本次未封口
- **Granted duration / 授予时长**: 不是自由小时。此作在灵感日报之后本地写成，不占用 03:17–04:17。
- **Experience duration / 体验时长**: open-ended / 开放
- **Sound / 声音**: 故意不发声。拆开是账页，不是气氛。Deliberate silence. Coming apart is a ledger, not an atmosphere.
