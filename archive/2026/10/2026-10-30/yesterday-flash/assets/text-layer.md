# 昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash

公开名字用「昨日闪光」。文字层不是三件作品。不贴源链接，不贴邮件原文。

## 中文

三下还亮着，只有一下能走进去。没站过的那种：活法说到一半，若必须再去一处没站过的点，就离开这张纸，不能跟说得圆的几种共用标题。后写的那一挪：画面已经能给人看了才补上的位置，是旁注，不是方法。合上才算：合上之后还能说完的三句才算数；有一句得打开才说得完，就还在架子上。

## English

Three glints are still lit; only one can be entered. The kind you have not stood: a way of living that must walk to a point never occupied leaves the sheet and cannot share the title with the ones that can be spoken round. The move written after: a position added once the picture is already readable is a caption, not a method. It counts only when closed: three sentences that can still be finished after the file is shut count; a sentence that needs the file open stays on the shelf.
