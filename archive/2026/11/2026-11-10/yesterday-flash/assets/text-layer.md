# 昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash

公开名字用「昨日闪光」。文字层不是三件作品。不贴源链接，不贴邮件原文。

## 中文

三下还亮着，只有一件能走进去。

嘴手之间：动手之前说过的那一句，和真正动的那一下，常常不是同一件事。这中间的距离平时没有人记。要记的只有一件，把说过的和做过的并排放着，在两条中间那条空栏里落一笔。那一笔不许压在任何一句话上，也不许从一栏连到另一栏。

翻一块地：拿一条走过的路，一次只翻动一样东西，门是开还是关，望不望得见，地标在不在。路塌不塌，塌向哪边。这样才分得出哪一处真的在管着走法，哪一处只是布景。借的是这个翻法，不是那个证明，这一页不写证明。

修补留痕：机器替人修过的地方要留着，压在成品下面当一层地层。观众看见的不只是做好的那一面，还有一层一层被机器动过的痕迹。再落一句单独的话，机器参与了之后，作者的痕迹在哪里。这一页只留三处，不做自动修补。

## English

Three glints are still lit. Only one can be entered.

Between Mouth and Hand: what was said before the hand moved and what the hand actually did are often not the same act. Nobody records the distance between them. Keep one thing. Put the said and the done side by side, and put one mark in the empty band between the two lines. The mark may not rest on either sentence, and it may not run from one band into another.

Flip One Field: take one path that was walked and change one thing at a time, the door open or shut, the landmark in sight or not. See whether the path collapses, and which way it falls. Only then can you tell the part that actually governs the walk from the part that is only scenery. Borrow the move, not the proof. This page does not argue the proof.

Repair Leaves Strata: keep what the machine repaired, pressed under the finished surface as a layer of ground. The viewer sees not only the finished side but layer upon layer of machine traces. Then set down one sentence alone. After the machine has taken part, where is the author's trace. Keep three places on this page and no automatic repair.
