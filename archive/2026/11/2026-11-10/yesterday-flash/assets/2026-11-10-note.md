# 嘴手之间 / Between Mouth and Hand

嵌在「昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash」里的一件。不是 05:00 生成艺术，不进 free-roam。

- **Free variable / 自由变量**: 嘴说过的和手真做的之间那段距离有没有人记 / whether anyone records the distance between what the mouth said and what the hand did
- **Intention / 发心**: 三处并排放着，中间各留一条空栏。落笔只能落在空栏里。那一笔不许压在任何一句话上，也不许从一栏连到另一栏。三处不合并，不加总。偏得多不等于错，偏得少也不等于对。
  Three places side by side, an empty band between each pair. The mark may only fall in the band. It may not rest on either sentence, and it may not run from one band into another. The three are never merged and never totalled. A wide gap is not an error, and a narrow gap is not a right answer.
- **Interaction / 交互**: 在任何一条空栏里落一笔。笔可以拖着走，也可以用方向键移。落笔之后那一处的后来会自己打开。三处都记下之后可以重记。没有分数，没有倒计时。
  Put one mark in any band. Drag it, or move it with the arrow keys. After the mark, that place's later opens by itself. Once all three are recorded, the page may be recorded again. No score. No countdown.
- **Afterimage / 余像**: 账页分开记三处，每处一行，嘴一句手一句，后来一句。没有总数。
  The ledger keeps the three places apart, one line each, the mouth's sentence, the hand's sentence, and the later. There is no total.
- **Source Day / 源日**: 2026-11-10
- **Crystallization Day / 结晶日**: 2026-11-10
- **Public seal / 封口**: next 06:35, not sealed in this run / 下一次 06:35，本次未封口
- **Granted duration / 授予时长**: 不是自由小时。此作在灵感日报之后本地写成，不占用 03:17–04:17。
- **Experience duration / 体验时长**: open-ended / 开放
- **Sound / 声音**: 故意不发声。留的是那段距离有没有被记下，不是气氛。Deliberate silence. What remains is whether the distance was recorded, not an atmosphere.
