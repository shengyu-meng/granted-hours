# 昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash

公开名字用「昨日闪光」。文字层不是三件作品。不贴源链接，不贴邮件原文。

## 中文

三下还亮着，只有一件能走进去。

一起转的平面：从脚底到树冠切开，每层只留那一高度真正切到的东西。换方向只能让所有层转同一个角度。唯一允许动手的地方，是让其中一层少转十度。少转的那一层露馅。露馅以后，纸必须承认，你以为站得住的山，是一组被要求一起转的平面。没有一层少转，这页不能收。

灯必须动过：纸上只写三样允许被改的实物，灯的高度，缝的宽，墙的转角。进来的人只能改这三样，再走同一条路。判断变了，这三样却还在原来的位置，这张纸作废。没有一件东西真的被拧过，涨了的判断不算数。

跟着进门的：每走一步，只写下下一间用不上、却还是跟进来的那一件，并写出名字。什么都没跟进来，这步还没走完。上一间整间都跟进来，也不算，那是搬家。名字要具体到能指着它说，就是这个。

## English

Three glints are still lit. Only one can be entered.

Co-rotated Planes: cut from the foot to the crown. Each layer may keep only what that height actually cuts. A change of direction may turn every layer only by the same angle. The only allowed hand is to let one layer lag by ten degrees. That layer gives the trick away. Afterward the page must admit that the mountain you thought could stand is a set of planes required to turn together. If no layer lags, the page cannot be kept.

The Lamp Must Have Moved: the page may name only three physical things that may be changed, the height of the lamp, the width of the seam, the corner of the wall. The person who enters may change only these three, then walk the same path again. If the judgment has changed and the three are still where they were, the page is void. If nothing was actually turned, a risen judgment does not count.

What Followed In: each step may write only the one thing the next room does not need and that still came in, and it must be named. If nothing followed in, the step is not finished. If the whole previous room followed in, that does not count. That is moving house. The name must be specific enough to point at.
