# 图不许知道 / The Image Must Not Know

嵌在「昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash」里的一件。不是 05:00 生成艺术，不进 free-roam。

- **Free variable / 自由变量**: 图有没有把外面的尺寸说出来 / whether the image speaks the measure written outside it
- **Intention / 发心**: 尺寸写在图外，图不许知道，这一张才留下。图把这个数说出来，图就退出，只留一句它从一开始就在等图纸。
  The image remains only while the measure stays outside and unspoken. If the image speaks that number, the image leaves, and only one sentence remains. It was waiting for the drawing from the start.
- **Interaction / 交互**: 把尺寸拖到图外，或点「写在外面」。图还不知道。点「停在外面」，这一张留下。点「让图说」，再把尺寸拖进图里，或点「说出来」：图退出，只留那一句。两种都试过以后，这张纸留下。可以换另一张纸。没有分数，没有倒计时。
  Drag the measure outside the image, or press Write it outside. The image does not yet know. Press Keep it outside, and this sheet remains. Press Let the image speak, then drag the measure into the image, or press Speak it. The image leaves, and only that sentence remains. After both gestures are tried, the sheet remains. Another sheet may be taken. No score. No countdown.
- **Afterimage / 余像**: 账页分开记图外、留下、作废。只写在外面的，图还不知道。停在外面的，这一张留下。说出来的，记下它从一开始就在等图纸。
  The ledger keeps the outside measure, the withholding, and the void apart. Written outside, the image does not yet know. Kept outside, the sheet remains. Spoken, it is recorded as having waited for the drawing from the start.
