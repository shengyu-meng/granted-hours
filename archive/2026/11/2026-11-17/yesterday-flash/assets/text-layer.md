# 昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash

公开名字用「昨日闪光」。文字层不是三件作品。不贴源链接，不贴邮件原文。

## 中文

三下还亮着，只有一件能走进去。

欠着的照看：承下来的那一块，要是离开之后的照看没有标上价，这块就是空的。你可以离开。价要写在离开之后。离开之前写的价不算。没标价就走，承下来的东西不成立。

睁眼前的误差：这一眼的误差在睁开之前就写下了。后来套上的好看的框，改不了那一笔。你看见的，不能修订先写好的误差。

没人握着的那一截：中间那一截要是没人握着，而一个人本来就能把它留住，这一截就是空的。不是共同托不住。是能留却没人握。

## English

Three glints are still lit. Only one can be entered.

Care Still Owed: an inherited part stays void unless the care after leaving is priced. You may leave. The price must be written after the leaving. A price written before leaving does not count. Leaving without a price does not hold the part.

Error Before the Eyes Open: the error of the glance is written before the eyes open. A flattering frame put on later cannot revise that line. What you see afterward cannot correct the error written first.

The Stretch No One Holds: the middle stretch is void if no one is holding it and one person could have kept it. It is not a shared failure to hold. It is a stretch one person could keep, left unheld.
