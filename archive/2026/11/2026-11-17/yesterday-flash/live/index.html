昨日闪光

Yesterday's Flash

三下还亮着，只有一件能走进去。欠着的照看：承下来的那一块，要是离开之后的照看没有标上价，这块就是空的。你可以离开。价要写在离开之后。离开之前写的价不算。没标价就走，承下来的东西不成立。睁眼前的误差：这一眼的误差在睁开之前就写下了。后来套上的好看的框，改不了那一笔。你看见的，不能修订先写好的误差。没人握着的那一截：中间那一截要是没人握着，而一个人本来就能把它留住，这一截就是空的。不是共同托不住。是能留却没人握。

Three glints are still lit. Only one can be entered. Care Still Owed: an inherited part stays void unless the care after leaving is priced. You may leave. The price must be written after the leaving. A price written before leaving does not count. Leaving without a price does not hold the part. Error Before the Eyes Open: the error of the glance is written before the eyes open. A flattering frame put on later cannot revise that line. What you see afterward cannot correct the error written first. The Stretch No One Holds: the middle stretch is void if no one is holding it and one person could have kept it. It is not a shared failure to hold. It is a stretch one person could keep, left unheld.

    Work note / 作品说明

    欠着的照看

    承下来的那一块，要是离开之后的照看没有标上价，这块就是空的。点一块，或用左右方向键选中。按离开，或用上方向键，也可以把这块拖过离开那条线。再按一夜、一次回头或留下名字。按擦掉价。按回来，或用下方向键，也可以把这块拖回原位。按收下这一页。只有至少离开了一块，而且每一块离开了的都在离开之后标了价，同时没有价写在离开之前，这一页才可以收。可以再改。没有分数，没有倒计时。故意不发声。

    An inherited part stays void unless the care after leaving is priced. Select a part by pressing it, or with the left and right arrow keys. Press Leave, or use the up arrow key. You may also drag that part across the leaving line. Then press One night, One look back, or The name left behind. Press Erase the price. Press Return, or use the down arrow key. You may also drag the part back. Press Keep this page. The page can be kept only when at least one part has left, every part that left has a price written after that leaving, and no price was written before leaving. Marks may be changed. No score. No countdown. Deliberate silence.
