# 昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash

公开名字用「昨日闪光」。文字层不是三件作品。不贴源链接，不贴邮件原文。

## 中文

三下还亮着，只有一下能走进去。接不上的那一笔：线头是你的，只画到一半。旁边可以写下另一只手看见了什么，不能让它接到终点。接到了，这条线退出，只留一句被代笔完成，所以不作数。静帧寄不走：能寄到另一间屋子的那句话，动一停就变成假的。写不出它为什么变假，这句话就只能标静帧，不能当出门的样子。没看着进来的：没看着进来的能力，可以取，可以排，不能起标题，也不能宣布今天算接触过。写成一份清单，这一页就失败了。

## English

Three glints are still lit; only one can be entered. The Stroke That Cannot Be Joined: the line head is yours, and it stops halfway. What the other hand saw may be written beside it. The other hand may not join the line to the end. If it does, the line leaves, and only one sentence remains: completed by another hand, so it does not count. A Still Cannot Be Sent: a sentence that can travel to another room becomes false the moment the motion stops. If that falsity cannot be written, the sentence may only be marked still, and may not leave as the thing itself. What Arrived Unwatched: a capacity that arrived while no one was watching may be kept or set aside. It may not title the page, and it may not declare that today counted as contact. Turned into an inventory, the page fails.
