# 接不上的那一笔 / The Stroke That Cannot Be Joined

嵌在「昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash」里的一件。不是 05:00 生成艺术，不进 free-roam。

- **Free variable / 自由变量**: 另一只手有没有接到终点 / whether the other hand reaches the end
- **Intention / 发心**: 线头是你的，只停在半路，这一笔才留下。另一只手可以写下看见，不能接到终点。接到了，这条线退出，所以不作数。
  The stroke remains only when its head is yours and it stops halfway. The other hand may write what it saw. It may not join the end. If it joins, the line leaves, and does not count.
- **Interaction / 交互**: 按住线头画出半笔，或点「起一笔」。笔不能越过不可接。半笔留下以后，把「看见」拖到旁边，或点「写下看见」。看见落到终点，不算写下。把「另一只手」拖过不可接、放到终点，或点「让它接」再点「接到终点」：线退出，只留不作数。把另一只手拖回不可接之前再松开，或点「停手」：这一笔留下。两种都试过以后，这张纸留下。可以换另一张纸。没有分数，没有倒计时。
  Draw a half stroke from the line head, or press Begin the stroke. The stroke cannot cross the unjoinable line. After the half stroke remains, drag Seen to the side, or press Write what was seen. If Seen lands on the end, it was not written. Drag The other hand past the unjoinable line onto the end, or press Let it join and then Join the end: the line leaves, and only the void sentence remains. Drag the other hand back before the unjoinable line and release, or press Stop the hand: the stroke remains. After both gestures are tried, the sheet remains. Another sheet may be taken. No score. No countdown.
- **Afterimage / 余像**: 账页分开记半笔、看见、拒绝、作废。只写下看见，线没有变长。拒绝的，这一笔留下。接到终点的，记下不作数。
  The ledger keeps the half stroke, the seeing, the refusal, and the void apart. A seeing note does not lengthen the line. A refusal keeps the stroke. A join to the end is recorded as not counting.
