# 昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash

公开名字用「昨日闪光」。文字层不是三件作品。不贴源链接，不贴邮件原文。

## 中文

三下还亮着，只有一件能走进去。

绕不开：只标视线真的打到的几个点。人可以跳过其中一处，直接走到结尾，结尾还是成立。成立，多半是因为结尾先画好了，不是被看见逼出来的。有一个点能跳过去，还到同一个结尾，这一页就不算。把那个点钉上。不要换一个更顺的结尾。

它更喜欢的那张：以后给你两版，左边只抄留下这一版时说的理由，右边放丢掉的那张。接着做的只能是右边。右边要是空的，说明没有真正的选择，这一页先停着。不要顺着留下的口味往下长。

第二部不许降：已经有的一个东西，要再发一次，方法不许变轻。变轻了，这两行就撕掉，不管第二次看起来多完整。第二次那个数只许写下，旁边不许解释。解释一写，就是在帮那个数找一个更舒服的说法。

## English

Three glints are still lit. Only one can be entered.

Cannot Be Skipped: mark only the points the glance actually hits. A person may skip one and still reach the ending. The ending still holds. It holds mostly because it was drawn first, not because seeing forced it. If one point can be skipped and the same ending still arrives, the page does not count. Nail that point. Do not swap in a smoother ending.

The Sheet It Preferred: when two versions are offered, the left side copies only the reason for keeping that one. The right side holds the discarded sheet. The only continuation is the right side. If the right side is empty, there was no real choice. Leave the page. Do not grow along the taste that kept the other one.

Second Must Not Lighten: something already in hand, released again, must not lighten its method. If the method lightens, tear up these two lines, however complete the second looks. The second number may be written and must not be explained. An explanation is helping that number find a more comfortable saying.
