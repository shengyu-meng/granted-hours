# 绕不开 / Cannot Be Skipped

嵌在「昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash」里的一件。不是 05:00 生成艺术，不进 free-roam。

- **Free variable / 自由变量**: 视线有没有真的打到那个点，还是结尾先画好了 / whether the glance actually hits the point, or the ending was drawn first
- **Intention / 发心**: 只标视线真的打到的几个点。人可以跳过其中一处，直接走到结尾，结尾还是成立。成立，多半是因为结尾先画好了，不是被看见逼出来的。有一个点能跳过去，还到同一个结尾，这一页就不算。把那个点钉上。不要换一个更顺的结尾。
  Mark only the points the glance actually hits. A person may skip one and still reach the ending. The ending still holds, mostly because it was drawn first, not because seeing forced it. If one point can be skipped and the same ending still arrives, the page does not count. Nail that point. Do not swap in a smoother ending.
- **Interaction / 交互**: 在视线里看下一处。可以点看下一处，可以拖着视线走，也可以用左右方向键。点必须被视线停住，才算打到。跳到结尾，若还有点没打到，结尾还是同一个，这一页不算。把那个点钉上。再看一次时，钉子还在，跳过钉子会被拦住。换一个更顺的结尾不会成功，结尾不动。可以再看一次。没有分数，没有倒计时。
  Look to the next place. Press Look on, drag the glance, or use the left and right arrow keys. A point counts only when the glance rests on it. Jump to the ending. If a point was not hit, the ending is still the same one, and the page does not count. Nail that point. On the next look the nail remains, and a jump over the nail is stopped. Swapping in a smoother ending does not succeed. The ending does not move. The page may be looked at again. No score. No countdown.
- **Afterimage / 余像**: 账页分开记打到的、钉上的、结尾。没有总数。
  The ledger keeps what was hit, what was nailed, and the ending apart. There is no total.
