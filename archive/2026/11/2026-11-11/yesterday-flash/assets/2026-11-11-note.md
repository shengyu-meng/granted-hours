# 原点钉死 / Origin Stays Nailed

嵌在「昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash」里的一件。不是 05:00 生成艺术，不进 free-roam。

- **Free variable / 自由变量**: 零是不是还钉在第一脚，人走远之后地面还清不清楚 / whether the zero stays nailed at the first footfall, and whether the ground stays as clear as the doorway after the person has walked far
- **Intention / 发心**: 第一脚站的地方就是零。人可以往前走，远处允许变粗，允许抖一下。零不许被随身拖走。走得很远，地面还是门口那种清楚，这一段就不算走过。
  The first footfall is zero. A person may walk on. Distance may coarsen, and it may shake. The zero may not travel with the walker. If the walk goes far and the ground is still as clear as the doorway, that stretch does not count as having been walked.
- **Interaction / 交互**: 在路上往前走。可以点往前走，可以拖着人走，也可以用左右方向键。零钉在第一脚。走到远处，地面变粗，允许抖一下，这一段才算走过。把零带走，或把钉子拖离门口，地面会回到门口那种清楚，这一段不作数。人还在门口时，零本来就在，不算带走。可以再走一次。没有分数，没有倒计时。
  Walk forward on the path. Press Step on, drag the person, or use the left and right arrow keys. The zero stays nailed at the first footfall. When the walk reaches the far ground, the grain coarsens and may shake, and that stretch counts as walked. Take the zero, or drag the nail off the doorway, and the ground returns to doorway clarity. That stretch does not count. If the person is still at the door, the zero was already there, and taking it does not count. The page may be walked again. No score. No countdown.
- **Afterimage / 余像**: 账页分开记零、远处、这一段。零被带走也不并进走过。没有总数。
  The ledger keeps the zero, the far ground, and this stretch apart. A zero that was taken along does not merge into a walk. There is no total.
