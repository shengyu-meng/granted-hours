昨日闪光

Yesterday's Flash

三下还亮着，只有一件能走进去。图下的小字：自己画的那条线，要是图下的小字没有点出哪几个点动过，这条线就是空的。图可以弯，页可以留白。没被点名的移动，只是纸上晃了一下，不算自己的曲线。没碰过就没有墙：墙只在碰上的那一下才出生。页要是空的，不是还没碰到，是皮早就画好了。空页不是等待，是预先画上的皮肤。认不出的那颗：旧分类的名字要是还能套上，这一页场地就不成立。认得出的那颗，还是旧名字。能留下的，只能是旧名字套不上的那一颗。

Three glints are still lit. Only one can be entered. Small Type Under the Figure: a curve you drew stays void unless the small type under the figure names the points that moved. The figure may bend. The page may stay blank. A movement the small type does not name is only a shiver on the paper. No Wall Before Contact: a wall is born only at contact. An empty page means the skin was already drawn, not that nothing has been touched yet. Emptiness is not waiting. It is a predrawn skin. The Unrecognizable One: if the old category name still fits, the site page is void. The one you can still recognize is still the old name. Only the one the old name cannot fit may remain.

    Work note / 作品说明

    图下的小字

    自己画的那条线，要是图下的小字没有点出哪几个点动过，这条线就是空的。点一个点，或用左右方向键选中。再按挪上、挪下，或用上下方向键，也可以在图上拖这个点。再按写入小字，或从字里拿掉。按回到原位，这个点不再算动过。按留下这张图，只有小字正好点出动过的点，这一页才可以收。可以再改。没有分数，没有倒计时。故意不发声。

    A curve you drew stays void unless the small type under the figure names the points that moved. Select a point by pressing it, or with the left and right arrow keys. Then press Nudge up or Nudge down, or use the up and down arrow keys. You may also drag that point on the figure. Then press Write into the small type, or Take it out of the type. Press Return to first place, and that point no longer counts as moved. Press Keep this figure. The page can be kept only when the small type names exactly the points that moved. Marks may be changed. No score. No countdown. Deliberate silence.
