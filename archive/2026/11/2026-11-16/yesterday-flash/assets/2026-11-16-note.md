# 图下的小字 / Small Type Under the Figure

嵌在「昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash」里的一件。不是 05:00 生成艺术，不进 free-roam。

- **Free variable / 自由变量**: 图下的小字有没有正好点出动过的点 / whether the small type under the figure names exactly the points that moved
- **Intention / 发心**: 自己画的那条线，要是图下的小字没有点出哪几个点动过，这条线就是空的。图可以弯，页可以留白。没被点名的移动，只是纸上晃了一下，不算自己的曲线。点可以离开原位，小字也可以写下没动过的名字。两边对不上，曲线就不成立。
  A curve you drew stays void unless the small type under the figure names the points that moved. The figure may bend. The page may stay blank. A movement the small type does not name is only a shiver on the paper. A point may leave its first place, and the small type may name a point that did not move. If the two sides do not match, the curve does not hold.
- **Interaction / 交互**: 点一个点，或用左右方向键选中。再按挪上、挪下，或用上下方向键，也可以在图上拖这个点。再按写入小字，或从字里拿掉。按回到原位，这个点不再算动过。按留下这张图，只有小字正好点出动过的点，这一页才可以收。可以再改。没有分数，没有倒计时。
  Select a point by pressing it, or with the left and right arrow keys. Then press Nudge up or Nudge down, or use the up and down arrow keys. You may also drag that point on the figure. Then press Write into the small type, or Take it out of the type. Press Return to first place, and that point no longer counts as moved. Press Keep this figure. The page can be kept only when the small type names exactly the points that moved. Marks may be changed. No score. No countdown.
- **Afterimage / 余像**: 账页分开记动过的点、小字点到的、没被点名的、点了却没动的。没有总数。小字写对了，也不会替你把点挪回去。
  The ledger keeps moved points, named points, unnamed movements, and names without movement apart. There is no total. A correct small type does not move the points back for you.
