昨日闪光

Yesterday's Flash

三下还亮着，只有一件能走进去。沉默冠名：先写三行已经有公开数字的，房子的轮廓，这一季的天气，表上说这里住着多少人。再写一行表上没有的，一扇对不上轮廓的门，或者一条人在走、地图上却不是路的缝。只有这一行许当标题。用旁边一条相似的街把这行补上数字，这张纸就作废。图不负责怕：左边只写倍数自己能说的话。右边写那句怕。中间空一行，只许填一种图和公告都给不了的证据。把换帅写成怕的证据，纸作废。纸尾若写成要做的那一手，也作废。砍完还齐：动手前只指定一对必须还在一起的东西。然后描述删一半，图层删一半，解释的句子也删一半。删完这一对还是同时落地，这次砍就留下来。如果又写了一句，专门把它们拽回去，这次不算砍。

Three glints are still lit. Only one can be entered. Silent Naming: first write three lines that already have public figures, the outline of the house, this season's weather, how many the table says live here. Then write one line the table does not have, a door that does not meet the outline, or a seam people walk that the map does not call a road. Only that line may be the title. If a similar street next door is used to put a figure on that line, the page is void. The Chart Does Not Answer for Fear: the left side may say only what a multiple can say for itself. The right side holds the sentence of fear. The blank line between them may hold only a kind of evidence that neither the chart nor the notice can give. Writing a change of leadership as proof of the fear voids the page. If the foot of the page is written as a hand to be played, it is void too. Still Together After the Cut: before the cut, name only one pair that must still land together. Then cut the description in half, the layers in half, and the explanatory sentences in half. If that pair still lands together, the cut is kept. A sentence written afterward to drag them back does not count as a cut.

      沉默冠名

      先写三行已经有公开数字的。再写一行表上没有的。点一行，或用左右方向键选中。按写门，或按写缝。按让这一行当标题，或用上方向键。只有那一行、并且没有借来的数字，才当得成标题。按借邻街的数，纸作废。按收回借数，或用下方向键。按收下这一页，或按回车。没有写出门或缝，不能收。借了数，不能收。没有分数，没有倒计时。故意不发声。

      First write three lines that already have public figures. Then write the one line the table does not have. Select a line by pressing it, or with the left and right arrow keys. Press Write the door, or Write the seam. Press Let this line be the title, or use the up arrow. Only that line, and only while it has no borrowed figure, may be the title. Press Borrow the neighbor's figure, and the page is void. Press Withdraw the borrowed figure, or use the down arrow. Press Keep this page, or press Enter. If the door or the seam has not been written, the page cannot be kept. If a figure was borrowed, the page cannot be kept. No score. No countdown. Deliberate silence.
