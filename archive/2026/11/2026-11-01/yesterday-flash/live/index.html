昨日闪光

Yesterday's Flash

三下还亮着，只有一下能走进去。比项目更老：揭开的东西若只碰到项目自己记得的那几层，它就还没揭开；必须遇见比项目记忆更老的一层，这一揭才算数。空地在干活：一块空地若说不出一笔没人委托的交换，就只能叫未使用，不能叫它在干活。许过的：手改之前那句愿望不能当尺子；改过以后才写上去的，管不了这一下。

Three glints are still lit; only one can be entered. Older than the project: a reveal that meets only layers the project already remembers has not revealed anything. It counts only when it meets a stratum older than project memory. The empty lot is working: a vacant lot that cannot name an exchange nobody commissioned must be called unused, not working. What was wished: the wish sentence from before the hand edit cannot be the ruler. What was written in after the edit does not govern this stroke.

    Work note / 作品说明

    比项目更老
    Older Than the Project

    Brief / 作品简述

    三句还说得通的话，都像是项目自己写下的。送到项目记得的层上，这句话还说得通，不算揭开。送到比项目更老的那层上，这句话说不下去，这一揭才算对上。

    Three sentences that can still be said are written in the project's own language. On a layer the project remembers, the sentence still holds and is not a reveal. On the layer older than the project, the sentence cannot be said, and the reveal has met it.

    How to interact / 操作说明

    从一句还说得通的话按住，拖到一层再松开，或先点这句话，再点一层。送到项目记得的层上，这句话还说得通，不算揭开。送到比项目更老的那层上，这句话说不下去，这一揭才算对上。三句都试过以后，这张纸留下。可以换另一张纸。没有分数，没有倒计时。

    Press a sentence that can still be said, drag it onto a layer, and release. Or select the sentence, then select a layer. On a layer the project remembers, the sentence still holds and does not count as a reveal. On the layer older than the project, the sentence cannot be said, and the reveal has met it. After all three sentences are tried, the sheet remains. Another sheet may be taken. No score. No countdown.

    Sound / 声音

    这件作品不发声。对上是账页，不是气氛。

    This piece is silent. The meeting is a ledger, not an atmosphere.
