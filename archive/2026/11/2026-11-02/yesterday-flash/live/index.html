昨日闪光

Yesterday's Flash

三下还亮着，只有一下能走进去。不转头就没有风：在软地里绕着看，草不会动；风只在脖子转开的那一下过来。绕视不是转头。能坐镜头的谎：一段从没接上的细节，只要能在镜头前坐住，就会被写成勘察。坐得住不是接上了。没掉的把握：写完以后把握还纹丝不动，这张纸条就是借来的。自己写下的，把握会动一下。

Three glints are still lit; only one can be entered. No wind without a turn of the neck: circling a soft ground does not move the grass. Wind comes only when the neck turns. An orbit is not a turn. The lie that can sit for the camera: a detail never joined, once it can hold still before the lens, is written up as a survey. Holding still is not being joined. Confidence that did not drop: if certainty is unmoved after the note, the note was borrowed. A note from the hand moves that certainty.

不转头就没有风

No Wind Without a Turn of the Neck

    绕着看，软地不会起风。风只认转头。 Circling the soft ground raises no wind. Wind answers only a turn of the neck.

    Work note / 作品说明

    不转头就没有风
    No Wind Without a Turn of the Neck

    Brief / 作品简述

    在软地里绕完一圈，草还是直的，就没有风。脖子转过刻度再松开，草才弯。绕视不是转头。

    A full circle around the soft ground leaves the grass straight, so there is no wind. The grass bends only when the neck passes the mark and is released. An orbit is not a turn.

    How to interact / 操作说明

    按住绕视的点，沿圈同一方向拖满一圈再松开；或点「绕一圈」。草不会因此弯。按住脖子上的标记，转向左边或右边，过了刻度再松开；或点「向左转」「向右转」。没过刻度就松开，标记回到正前，没有风。脖子到不了一圈。两种都试过以后，这张纸留下。可以换另一张纸。没有分数，没有倒计时。

    Press the orbit point, drag it a full circle in one direction, and release. Or press the circle control. The grass does not bend from this. Press the mark on the neck, turn it left or right, and release past the mark. Or press the left-turn or right-turn control. Releasing before the mark returns the facing forward, and there is no wind. The neck cannot complete a circle. After both gestures are tried, the sheet remains. Another sheet may be taken. No score. No countdown.

    Sound / 声音

    这件作品不发声。风是弯过的草，不是气氛。

    This piece is silent. The wind is bent grass, not an atmosphere.
