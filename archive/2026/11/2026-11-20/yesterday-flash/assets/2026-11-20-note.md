# 替身半径 / The Stand-in Radius

嵌在「昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash」里的一件。不是 05:00 生成艺术，不进 free-roam。

- **Free variable / 自由变量**: 这条线跟着脚走以后，远处还装不装成真的 / whether, once the line follows the foot, the far things are still pretending to be true
- **Intention / 发心**: 脚底下那一小块才许有量过的东西。眼睛还看得到、人还没走到的，必须自报是替身，并写出缺的那块骨头。来的人指不出这条线，这页作废。
  Only the small patch underfoot may hold what was measured. What the eye still sees, and no one has walked to, must confess it is a stand-in and name the missing bone. If the person who arrives cannot point out this line, the page is void.
- **Interaction / 交互**: 点一处，或用左右方向键选中。井口、石缝先在脚底下。北树、东坡先在远处，看起来和近处一样，还没写缺的骨头。拖动脚，或按往西、往东、往北、往南，线跟着脚走。走进一处，它才许被量。按记下量过，或用上方向键：只在脚底下记得住。远处按记下量过，页子不收这一量。远处按自报替身，或在远处时用上方向键：它写下自己是替身，并写出缺的那块骨头。脚底下不必自报。量过的一处若被走出线外，这一量不许留在远处。按收回这一量，或用下方向键，先收回，再让它自报。点跟着脚走的那条线，或聚焦后按回车，才算指出。眼睛的圈不跟着脚，指它不算。按收下这一页。只有线被指出，脚底下至少有一处量过，远处每一处都已自报并且没有量过的东西留在线外，这一页才收得下。按另写一页，重新开始，上一页发生过的事留在余像里。没有分数，没有倒计时。
  Select a place by pressing it, or with the left and right arrow keys. The well mouth and the stone crack begin underfoot. The north tree and the east slope begin far away, looking like the near places, with the missing bone not yet written. Drag the foot, or press West, East, North, South. The line follows the foot. A place may be measured only after you have walked to it. Press Record as measured, or use the up arrow, only while it is underfoot. Recording a far place is not kept. Press Confess as stand-in, or use the up arrow while it is far: it writes that it is a stand-in and names the missing bone. What is underfoot does not need to confess. If a measured place is walked outside the line, that measurement may not remain far away. Press Withdraw this measurement, or use the down arrow, then let it confess. Point the line that follows the foot, or focus it and press Enter. The eye's circle does not follow the foot, and pointing it does not count. Press Keep this page. The page can be kept only when the line has been pointed out, at least one place underfoot has been measured, and every far place has confessed with no measurement left outside the line. Press Write another page to begin again. What happened to the last page stays in the afterimage. No score. No countdown.
- **Afterimage / 余像**: 账页分开记脚底下量过的、自报替身的、还装着像真的、量过却离开脚底的。线被指出，或没有。没有总数。走进去以后，远处还算不算真，页子不回答。
  The ledger keeps what was measured underfoot, what confessed as a stand-in, what is still pretending to be true, and what was measured and then left the foot, apart. The line was pointed out, or it was not. There is no total. Whether the far ground still counts as true after you walk there is not answered by the page.
