Yesterday's Flash

三下还亮着，只有一件能走进去。画面不许改：先有一张预测的画面。人可以走一步，步子落到画面没画的地方。画面必须跟这一步对不上。对上了，这一页就是导览。改画面去凑步子，不会成功。把步子改到画面上，缝就没了，这一页不算。留下那条缝。先吃掉的：斜坡上最先能吃掉的那一层，才配给斜坡起名。那一层没被吃掉，标题还停在纸面上，这一页就是海报。先把那一层吃掉。不要先给斜坡一个好看的名字。住不进这缸的：新来的活物，水要是跟缸里已有的场面一致，它就是空的。水必须跟场面顶一下，它才算住进来。水顺着场面，就先别把那只活物写上。

Three glints are still lit. Only one can be entered. The Picture May Not Change: a predicted picture is already there. A person may take a step. The step lands where the picture did not draw. The picture must stay unreconciled with that step. If they meet, the page is a tour. Amending the picture to meet the step does not succeed. Bend the step onto the picture, and the gap is gone. The page does not count. Leave the gap. The First Layer Eaten: the first layer on the slope that can be eaten is the one that may title the slope. If that layer is not eaten, the title stays on the paper, and the page is a poster. Eat that layer first. Do not give the slope a good-looking name first. What Cannot Live in This Tank: a new creature whose water agrees with the scene already in the tank is empty. The water must contradict the scene, or it has not moved in. If the water follows the scene, do not write the creature in yet.

    画面不许改

    The Picture May Not Change

    自由变量 / Free variable

    预测的画面跟这一步对不对得上。Whether the predicted picture stays unreconciled with the step.

    发心 / Intention

    先有一张预测的画面。人可以走一步，步子落到画面没画的地方。画面必须跟这一步对不上。对上了，这一页就是导览。改画面去凑步子，不会成功。把步子改到画面上，缝就没了，这一页不算。留下那条缝。

    A predicted picture is already there. A person may take a step. The step lands where the picture did not draw. The picture must stay unreconciled with that step. If they meet, the page is a tour. Amending the picture to meet the step does not succeed. Bend the step onto the picture, and the gap is gone. The page does not count. Leave the gap.

    交互 / Interaction

    在地上走一步。可以点走一步，可以拖着步子走，也可以用左右方向键。画面已经画好，拖画面、点画面、或按改画面，都不会成功。步子停在画面预测的那一处，这一页就是导览，不算。把步子改到画面上，缝没了，直到再走一次。再走一次时，画面还在原处。可以再走一次。没有分数，没有倒计时。

    Take a step on the ground. Press Step on, drag the step, or use the left and right arrow keys. The picture is already drawn. Dragging it, pressing it, or pressing Amend the picture does not succeed. If the step rests on the place the picture predicted, the page is a tour and does not count. Bend the step onto the picture, and the gap is gone until the page is walked again. On the next walk the picture is still where it was drawn. The page may be walked again. No score. No countdown.

    余像 / Afterimage

    账页分开记画面、步子、缝。没有总数。

    The ledger keeps the picture, the step, and the gap apart. There is no total.

    Source Day 2026-11-13. Crystallization Day 2026-11-13. Open-ended. Deliberate silence.
