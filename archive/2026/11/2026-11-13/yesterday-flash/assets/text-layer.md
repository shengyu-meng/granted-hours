# 昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash

公开名字用「昨日闪光」。文字层不是三件作品。不贴源链接，不贴邮件原文。

## 中文

三下还亮着，只有一件能走进去。

画面不许改：先有一张预测的画面。人可以走一步，步子落到画面没画的地方。画面必须跟这一步对不上。对上了，这一页就是导览。改画面去凑步子，不会成功。把步子改到画面上，缝就没了，这一页不算。留下那条缝。

先吃掉的：斜坡上最先能吃掉的那一层，才配给斜坡起名。那一层没被吃掉，标题还停在纸面上，这一页就是海报。先把那一层吃掉。不要先给斜坡一个好看的名字。

住不进这缸的：新来的活物，水要是跟缸里已有的场面一致，它就是空的。水必须跟场面顶一下，它才算住进来。水顺着场面，就先别把那只活物写上。

## English

Three glints are still lit. Only one can be entered.

The Picture May Not Change: a predicted picture is already there. A person may take a step. The step lands where the picture did not draw. The picture must stay unreconciled with that step. If they meet, the page is a tour. Amending the picture to meet the step does not succeed. Bend the step onto the picture, and the gap is gone. The page does not count. Leave the gap.

The First Layer Eaten: the first layer on the slope that can be eaten is the one that may title the slope. If that layer is not eaten, the title stays on the paper, and the page is a poster. Eat that layer first. Do not give the slope a good-looking name first.

What Cannot Live in This Tank: a new creature whose water agrees with the scene already in the tank is empty. The water must contradict the scene, or it has not moved in. If the water follows the scene, do not write the creature in yet.
