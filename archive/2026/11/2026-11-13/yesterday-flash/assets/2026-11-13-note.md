# 画面不许改 / The Picture May Not Change

嵌在「昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash」里的一件。不是 05:00 生成艺术，不进 free-roam。

- **Free variable / 自由变量**: 预测的画面跟这一步对不对得上 / whether the predicted picture stays unreconciled with the step
- **Intention / 发心**: 先有一张预测的画面。人可以走一步，步子落到画面没画的地方。画面必须跟这一步对不上。对上了，这一页就是导览。改画面去凑步子，不会成功。把步子改到画面上，缝就没了，这一页不算。留下那条缝。
  A predicted picture is already there. A person may take a step. The step lands where the picture did not draw. The picture must stay unreconciled with that step. If they meet, the page is a tour. Amending the picture to meet the step does not succeed. Bend the step onto the picture, and the gap is gone. The page does not count. Leave the gap.
- **Interaction / 交互**: 在地上走一步。可以点走一步，可以拖着步子走，也可以用左右方向键。画面已经画好，拖画面、点画面、或按改画面，都不会成功。步子停在画面预测的那一处，这一页就是导览，不算。把步子改到画面上，缝没了，直到再走一次。再走一次时，画面还在原处。可以再走一次。没有分数，没有倒计时。
  Take a step on the ground. Press Step on, drag the step, or use the left and right arrow keys. The picture is already drawn. Dragging it, pressing it, or pressing Amend the picture does not succeed. If the step rests on the place the picture predicted, the page is a tour and does not count. Bend the step onto the picture, and the gap is gone until the page is walked again. On the next walk the picture is still where it was drawn. The page may be walked again. No score. No countdown.
- **Afterimage / 余像**: 账页分开记画面、步子、缝。没有总数。
  The ledger keeps the picture, the step, and the gap apart. There is no total.
