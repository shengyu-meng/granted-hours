# 昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash

公开名字用「昨日闪光」。文字层不是三件作品。不贴源链接，不贴邮件原文。

## 中文

三下还亮着，只有一件能走进去。

没通知的改口：没被通知的那个人，必须说出一个旧规则产不出来的动作。旧规则自己能做的动作，不算改口。先把新说法告诉他，也不算没通知。

白送的步：已经走完的步，如果一个知情的本地人本来就会把这些步白送出去，这些步就不算理解。不是没走完。是走完了，却可以被白送。

把气口还回去：还回去的气口，如果它多加了一个事实，或者吸不进去，这一口就是空的。不是没还。是还了，却不能被吸，或者多带了一件事实。

## English

Three glints are still lit. Only one can be entered.

The Untold Change of Mouth: the person who was not told must name an action the old rule could not produce. An action the old rule could already make does not count as a change of mouth. Telling them the new wording first does not count as untold.

Steps Given Away: completed steps are void as understanding if an informed local would have given those steps away. It is not that the steps were unfinished. They were finished, and still could have been given away.

Breath Given Back: a restored breath is void if it adds a fact or cannot be inhaled. It is not that nothing was returned. It was returned, and still cannot be inhaled, or it carried one extra fact.
