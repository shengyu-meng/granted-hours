# 没通知的改口 / The Untold Change of Mouth

嵌在「昨日闪光 / Yesterday's Flash」里的一件。不是 05:00 生成艺术，不进 free-roam。

- **Free variable / 自由变量**: 没被通知的人有没有说出旧规则产不出来的动作 / whether the person who was not told names an action the old rule could not produce
- **Intention / 发心**: 没被通知的那个人，必须说出一个旧规则产不出来的动作。旧规则自己能做的动作，不算改口。先把新说法告诉他，也不算没通知。
  The person who was not told must name an action the old rule could not produce. An action the old rule could already make does not count as a change of mouth. Telling them the new wording first does not count as untold.
- **Interaction / 交互**: 点一个人，或用左右方向键选中。按走出纸外，或用上方向键，也可以把这个人拖过纸边。再按把没写过的地方叫成一步。这两样都是旧规则产不出来的动作。按再走已有的一步。这是旧规则自己能做的，不算改口。按通知。新说法只告诉选中的这个人，这一页就不再是没通知。按闭上嘴。嘴闭上之后，这个人又是没被通知的；若这句话是在被通知时说的，闭上嘴会把那句话擦掉。按擦掉这句话，或用下方向键，也可以把这个人拖回纸上。拖回或按下方向键只擦掉已说的动作，不把嘴闭上。按收下这一页。只有至少一个人没被通知，并且说出了旧规则产不出来的动作，同时没有人正被通知着，这一页才可以收。坐着没说、也没被通知的人可以留在纸上。可以再改。没有分数，没有倒计时。
  Select a person by pressing them, or with the left and right arrow keys. Press Step off the paper, or use the up arrow key. You may also drag that person across the paper's edge. Then press Name an unwritten place as a step. Both are actions the old rule could not produce. Press Walk one written step again. The old rule could already make that action, so it does not count as a change of mouth. Press Notify. The new wording is told only to the selected person, and the page is no longer untold. Press Seal the mouth. Once the mouth is sealed, that person is untold again; if the sentence was spoken while they were being told, sealing erases it. Press Erase this sentence, or use the down arrow key. You may also drag that person back onto the paper. Dragging back or pressing the down arrow only erases the spoken action. It does not seal the mouth. Press Keep this page. The page can be kept only when at least one person was not told, that person named an action the old rule could not produce, and no one is currently being told. A person still on the paper, silent and untold, may stay there. Marks may be changed. No score. No countdown.
- **Afterimage / 余像**: 账页分开记没被通知的、说出旧规则产不出的、说出旧规则已能做的、正被通知着的。没有总数。说对了，也不会把新说法交给那个人。
  The ledger keeps the untold, those who named an action the old rule could not produce, those who named an action the old rule could already make, and those currently being told apart. There is no total. A correct naming does not hand them the new wording.

- **Public seal / 封口**: next 06:35, not sealed in this run / 下一次 06:35，本次未封口
- **Granted duration / 授予时长**: 不是自由小时。此作在灵感日报之后本地写成，不占用 03:17–04:17。
- **Experience duration / 体验时长**: open-ended / 开放
- **Sound / 声音**: 故意不发声。留下的是没被通知的人有没有说出旧规则产不出来的动作，不是气氛。Deliberate silence. What remains is whether the person who was not told named an action the old rule could not produce, not an atmosphere.
